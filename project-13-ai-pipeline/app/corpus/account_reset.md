# Student Account and Password Reset Policy

A staff member may reset a student account password from the Faculty Portal under Tools then Account Reset.
The reset link sent to the student expires after 30 minutes.
Students in grades 6 through 12 must change the temporary password at first login.
Staff may not reset another staff member's password. Staff password resets go to the IT Help Desk.
Accounts locked after five failed login attempts unlock automatically after 15 minutes.
The IT Help Desk is open from 7:30 AM to 4:00 PM on school days.
