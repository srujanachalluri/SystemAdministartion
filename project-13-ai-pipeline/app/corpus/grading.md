# Grading and Progress Report Policy

Quarter grades are due in the student information system by 4:00 PM on the Monday after the quarter closes.
Progress reports are published to families at the midpoint of each quarter.
A grade of Incomplete converts to the earned grade after 10 school days unless the teacher files an extension.
Teachers may not change a posted quarter grade without written approval from the Principal.
The gradebook weighting is 60 percent assessments, 30 percent classwork, and 10 percent participation.
