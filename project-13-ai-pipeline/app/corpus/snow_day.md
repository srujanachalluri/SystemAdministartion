# Snow Day and Emergency Closing Procedure

The decision to close the school for weather is made by the Head of School by 5:30 AM.
Staff are notified through the SchoolMessenger phone tree and by email.
Families are notified by text message and on the school website banner.
On a snow day, all athletic practices and evening facility rentals are cancelled automatically.
Teachers do not report to the building on a full closing day.
A two-hour delay means classes begin at 10:00 AM and morning bus routes run two hours late.
