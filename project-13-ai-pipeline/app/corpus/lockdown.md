# Lockdown and Secure Building Procedure

There are two levels. A Secure Building means exterior doors are locked and instruction continues normally inside.
A Lockdown means doors are locked, lights are off, students move out of sight lines, and no one opens a door for anyone.
Only the Head of School or the designated administrator on duty may announce or release a lockdown.
A lockdown is released only by an in-person all clear from an administrator, never by intercom and never by text message.
Teachers take attendance and report it through the safety app once the room is secure.
Staff must not evacuate the building during a lockdown unless directed in person by law enforcement.
