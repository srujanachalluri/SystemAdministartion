## Notes on the filled cells

- Row 4 is the strongest control in the set: at CRI the audit-log write happens
  BEFORE the action executes, and if the write fails the action does not run.
  Logging is a precondition, not a side effect.
- Row 7's stop authority is deliberately asymmetric: turning autonomy UP needs a
  named signature; turning it DOWN needs nothing. Any of the nine may pull the
  kill switch without asking permission first.
- Row 8's cap is a HARD STOP. A cap that only sends an email is a notification,
  not a guardrail.
- Rows 1-3 exist because the most likely supply-chain compromise for a nine-person
  team is not a nation-state — it is an unpinned dependency nobody re-read.
