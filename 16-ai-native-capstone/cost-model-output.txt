[1] VRAM SIZING - self-hosted case-note lane (W1)
    model shape       : ~70B params, 1 byte/param (FP8/INT8), 80 layers, 8 KV heads (GQA)
    context / concurrency : 8,192 tokens x 24 in flight

    weights                           70.0 GB
    KV cache per request              2.68 GB
    KV cache x24  concurrent        64.4 GB   <-- scales with LOAD, not model size
    runtime overhead (12%)             16.1 GB
    --------------------------------------------
    TOTAL VRAM REQUIRED              150.6 GB
    GPUs needed @ 80 GB                 2
    KV cache is 48% of live VRAM. Size the CACHE, not just the model.
    Headroom check: 2 GPUs hold ~27 concurrent requests at 8,192 ctx.
